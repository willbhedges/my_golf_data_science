
# Spirit Hollow tee strategy, v2: what changed, the evidence, and the verdict

Black tees, Spirit Hollow GC. Driver 275/300, 2-wood 265/280, 4-wood 255/270 carry/total (at 70°F), Long iron 210,
Mid iron 185, and a **hypothetical ~235 hybrid** (not in the bag; shown, never picked). Dispersion is
centred on 70 / 65 / 54 yds (95% lateral width). The live wind page and `output/final_decision.json`
are untouched. Everything here lives in `spirit_hollow/v2/`. Generated by `make_report.py` from
`output/v2_decisions.json` (GitHub Actions, 64 prior draws x 3,000 swings per hole),
`output/calibration.json` and `output/reverse_regret.json`.

Prior: 64 scrambled-Sobol draws over 30 dimensions. Width is lognormal (median 70, 90% 62-80) with
width ratios as above. Carry: common N(0, 5) plus per-club N(0, 3). Depth window ×LN(1.25, 0.2). Also
drawn: soft roll, rough roll, trees, lost-ball edge/deep, mishit rates, ρ, κ, bias, temperature
offset and coefficient, rough/sand multipliers, distance slope, first cut, punch-out advance/failure,
line-of-sight severity, tree-restricted cost, and a map jitter (each polygon ±2.5 yds). Each draw uses
its own 3,000 swings, shared by all clubs and aims.

Decision rule: pick the real club with the lowest prior-averaged EV at its robust aim. Robust aim =
argmin of the **prior-averaged** curve, not the central draw's.

Par 5s and the next shot: the strokes-to-hole tables already price the next shot's lie, distance and
dispersion, including going for a par 5 or laying up (that is how the baseline is measured). The
amateur slope term makes each yard closer worth more than it is for a tour player.

## 2. Literature used this round (research.md has the rest)

* **Temperature:** Andrew Rice/TrackMan (GOLF 2018-19): ~2-2.5 yds per 10°F for driver; humidity
  negligible. Secondhand TrackMan figure: 1.33-1.66 yds per 10°F by club. Titleist: 1.5% per 20°F.
  [GOLF](https://golf.com/instruction/how-cooler-weather-impacts-golf-carry-distance/),
  [Golf News Net](https://thegolfnewsnet.com/golfnewsnetteam/2021/12/01/how-much-shorter-does-a-golf-ball-travel-in-cold-weather-124626/)
* **Mishit distance:** Golf Digest 2026 robot driver test: ¾-in toe strikes average about −5 yds,
  heel strikes −12 to −15 yds.
  [Golf Digest](https://www.golfdigest.com/story/drivers-2026-robotic-testing-data-gear-questions)
* **Recovery / tee-shot outcomes:** ShotLink via GOLF: tour recovery tee balls ~2.3% of drives, ~0.85
  strokes each; weekend golfers ~16% of tee shots into recovery.
  [GOLF](https://golf.com/instruction/the-pros-save-strokes-by-missing-tee-shots-in-the-right-places-you-can-too/)
* **First cut:** strokes-gained practice treats intermediate rough as near-fairway (Minton). At the 2017
  US Open the first cut cost ~½ stroke vs ¾ for the second.
  [Minton](https://apps.roanoke.edu/minton/SG%20Implement.html),
  [USGA](https://victory.usopen.com/2017/articles/strokes-gained-thru-36.html)
* **One-way vs two-way miss:** Titleist / Practical Golf forum: aim-off only pays for a reliable one-way
  shape, and good drivers miss about equally left and right. v2 therefore keeps a symmetric centre and
  tests a fade and a draw as sensitivities.
  [Titleist](https://www.titleist.eu/instruction/eliminate-one-side)
* **Broadie on amateur dispersion:** amateurs are about twice as wide in angle as tour players
  (Golfmetrics, 43k amateur shots). The per-group table could not be retrieved (columbia.edu is
  blocked here), so the 70-yd centre stays the golfer's own and 82 yds (σ ≈ 21, fitted in research.md
  to Shot Scope/Arccos 0-5 hcp data) is reported as a sensitivity.
* **Water relief, lost ball:** Rules 17.1 (reference = where the ball last crossed the edge) and 18.2
  (stroke and distance). Model Local Rule E-5 (2 strokes, fairway drop) costs within ~0.05 of S&D here.


## 3. Verdict (corrected roll: every club rolls to its stated total; no slope kick)

**Correction.** The golfer's "soft" note was about kick and bounce, not total roll. The deciding run
(GitHub Actions 39011577014) rolls each club to its stated total on level fairway: 25 / 15 / 15 yds,
×DEM slope factor 0.7-1.3, ±20% between worlds, ±25% per swing. An earlier v2 run had the ball stop
dead; it is kept only as a sensitivity (`output/v2_decisions_stopdead.json`, the "stops dead" column).

**v2 is the better model, and the page is built** (`spirit_hollow/v2/wind_page/index.html`, to publish as
a NEW artifact). It is the same engine (it reproduces v1 to ≤ 0.007 strokes in v1's world). It adds
the forecast temperature at each hole, ground yards, Black-tee elevation, NAIP-checked greens, aims to
0.5 yd within the checked ±40 corridor, rules-based penalty pricing and every club on every hole.

With the corrected roll, **v2 mostly confirms v1.** The club differs on 3 of 14 Black-tee holes (10,
14 and 16, the last a tie), plus 9 from the Gold tee. Under v2, v1's plays cost ≈ 0.12 strokes per Black-tee round. If
v1's world were true, v2's plays would cost ≈ 0.14. So the stakes are small both ways.

### Holes that change vs v1, and why (change ladder below)

* **10: Driver → 4-wood 6.5 R** (0.057, beats the Driver in 86% of worlds).
  * v1's Driver came from its Bayesian run's wider 4-wood pattern (59.5 vs the stated 54). At the stated
    widths v1's own model already picks the 4-wood (ladder step 0).
  * Trees left block the line to the green on this par 5. The line-of-sight term moves the aim from
    1.5 R to 7-8 R.
* **14: 2-wood 25 L (at v1's search limit) → Driver 32.5 L** (0.024, 73%).
  * v1 could not aim past 25 L. The Driver's best aim is 30-35 L.
  * The scratch-5 table is what makes it a Driver (ladder step 9). Under the PGA Tour table it is a
    4-wood.
* **9 Gold: 2-wood → Driver 11 L** (0.046, 77%). The 48°F carry loss first makes it a 4-wood. The
  line-of-sight term then makes it a Driver aimed less far left (18.5 L → 10.5 L). Gold tee only.
* **16: 4-wood (tie) → Driver 8.5 L, still a tie** (0.001 ± 0.004; cross-fitted −0.001). Either club;
  the 4-wood under the tour table, a pattern ≥ 72 yds, or a draw.
* **Aims only (same club):**
  * 9 Black 4-wood 12.5 L → 7 L: the line-of-sight term; the pond still guards the right.
  * 2 Black 17.5 L → 14.5 L.
  * 13 10 L → 13 L.
  * 11 Long iron 2.5 R → 4.5 R.
  * 17 2.5 R → 1.5 L: trees on the line to the green.
* **Unchanged:** 1 (4-wood), 2 Gold, 4 (Driver), 5 (4-wood), 6 (2-wood, a 0.003 tie with the Driver),
  8 (4-wood), 11 (Long iron), 13 (Driver, a 0.006 tie with the 4-wood), 17 (Driver), 18 (2-wood,
  0.010 over the 4-wood).

**The single biggest modelling choice is the scoring table.** With the PGA Tour table (as v1 and DECADE
use), 6 → 2-wood and 13, 14, 16 → 4-wood. With the scratch-5 amateur table, which gives a smaller rough
penalty and makes each yard closer worth more, they lean Driver. Both readings are within ~0.02 strokes
on those holes.

### Hypothetical ~235 hybrid

With full roll it **would be the play on 11** (0.093 better than the Long iron: it gets closer to the
creek at ~276-282 without reaching it) and **on 9 Black** (0.031 better than the 4-wood). It is
level on 10 (+0.002). Elsewhere it costs 0.02-0.24. If the golfer ever carries one, those are its
holes.

### Things that look wrong in v1 (still true after the correction)

1. **Aims at the search limit.** Hole 14's 2-wood and Driver sat at −25 (v1 searched ±25). v2's best
   Driver aim there is 30-35 L. The v1 wind grid searched ±30 and never checked its limits.
2. **Width ratios.** The final run's prior centred the 2-wood/4-wood at 0.94/0.85 of the driver
   (70/66/59.5), not the stated 70/65/54. That is what makes v1 pick Driver on 10.
3. **Irons only on 11 and 13.** v2 offers every club everywhere. Irons only matter on 11.
4. **Penalty pricing.** A lost ball cost 2 + rough at the landing distance, not stroke and distance.
   Water used the landing spot, not where the ball crossed in.
5. **No temperature.** At 44-52°F the driver carries ~4 yds less.
6. **Geometry** (parity engineer, fixed in `geom.py`): elevation measured from the screenshot tee
   (driver bonus on 10: +11.2 → +6.0), app yards ≠ ground yards (6 ×0.968, 16 ×1.024), and greens on 6
   and 10 that sit on the front edge in NAIP.
7. **Unmapped ground scored as rough.** With a wider search the optimiser cut across unmapped ground
   (18 right of the creek; 9 Black over the pond). v2 limits aims to ±40 and scores untraced ground
   beyond the hand-checked ±55-yd corridor as half rough, half trees. Worth checking on the course:
   is the strip right of the creek on 18 in play from the Black tee?

### Robustness

* **Aims:** no pick at a search limit. Aim windows (within 0.005 strokes) are 4.5-8 yds wide.
  * The robust (prior-averaged) aim is within 1.5 yds of the central-world aim on 12 of 16 holes.
  * 9 Black: robust 7 L vs central 3.5 L. Averaging over uncertain carry and width keeps it further
    from the pond.
  * 6 and 18: the central world picks a different club (Driver 9.5 L; 4-wood 9 L). Both are ties.
* **Monte Carlo:** cross-fitted margins equal the in-sample ones to ≤ 0.003 (no winner's curse).
* **Temperature:** forecast 44-52°F through the round. 70°F tips 6, 9 Gold and 18 toward the 2-wood;
  8°F colder tips 13 to the 4-wood. The page re-solves every hole for the forecast at the time you
  reach it.
* **Wind page:** at the Black tees a handful of cells in 20-25 mph winds put the landing line at the
  ±40 map limit (the forecast is ≤ 13 mph). Tee sets moved 40+ yds back or 20+ up land partly off the
  hand-checked map; trust those less.
