## Definitions

* **hole greens**: perceived greens with `kind == hole` vs OSM greens a golf=hole way ends on (18 m). A green matches when its centroid is within 6 m of the OSM polygon. Practice greens (OSM greens no hole ends on) called `hole` count as false positives; perceived `practice` / `uncertain` greens are not in the hole set.
* **tee complexes**: recall = share of OSM hole-way starts with a box of a perceived complex (p >= 0.5) within 30 m; precision = share of perceived complexes with a box within 25 m of an OSM tee box or hole start, on courses whose OSM tees number >= 1.5 per hole.
* **fairways / bunkers / water / buildings / parking**: a perceived object is correct when >= 30 % of its pixels fall in the OSM class; an OSM object is found when >= 30 % of it is covered by perceived objects (course boundary + 40 m).
* **cart paths**: length within 4 m of an OSM cart path / footpath / in-course service way (and vice versa), inside the course + 40 m, on courses with >= 1.5 km of OSM paths per 18 holes. **Connectivity**: for each consecutive hole pair whose green-end -> next-tee is connected by OSM paths (attach 45 m), the perceived graph connects them too with a route <= 2 x the OSM route + 60 m.
* **edge accuracy** (ordering evidence): for each OSM hole n with hole n+1 mapped and its green perceived, the perceived tee complex with the highest `p_next` from that green is within 30 m of hole n+1's start.
* **clubhouse**: distance from the perceived clubhouse point to the OSM golf=clubhouse / building=clubhouse (or a building named club house / pro shop).
