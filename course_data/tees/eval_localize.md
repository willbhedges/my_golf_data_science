## Notes

* course_data/truth/ (Coldwater, Veenker): the hand corridors are framed on the package's own Blue tee (NOTES.md), so they hold no independent tee truth; both courses are scored in C1 instead.
* Spirit Hollow truth = the 18Birdies tee moved straight up the app's line by BLACK_TEE_FORWARD. On the five worst keys (1, 2B, 14, 16, 17) the error is across the line (21-29 yds), not along it, and on 1 / 14 / 17 the truth point is 6-16 yds outside every OSM tee box (trees / bank): the real Black box is beside the app's line, so no pad-based method can get closer there; both methods put Black on the same box.
* sigma_yd is the 1-sigma radial uncertainty (assignment spread + within-pad + source); 'within 1 / 2 sigma' is the share of truth errors inside it.
