 Golf 
 in Burlington, IA
From our championship golf course, ranked as the #1 Best Course You Can Play in Iowa by Golfweek, right through the comfortable Lodge and Cabin style room accommodations, Lambo's casual dining and the Shankopotamus Golf Academy powered by Toptracer Range, we honor the history and traditions of the game and nurture the spirit of those who play.

 Toptracer Range 
 You can't play golf all night, we turn off the lights at midnight. But until then, the Old Course, Spyglass and several more great courses are teed up for you at the Shankopotamus Golf Academy powered by Toptracer Range.

 Accommodations 
 Spread out, relax and recuperate in style at Spirit Hollow. Whether you choose our Lodge room accommodations or the 4-bedroom Cabins that accommodate up to 8 players, you'll find yourself recharged and ready to play your best.

 Lambo's Casual Fare 
 The delicious meals and casual ambiance served up daily at Lambo's make its 40 big-screen TVs and video wall the third best reason to stay awhile. Fresh food, is served with our unique Spirit Hollow hospitality every day.
