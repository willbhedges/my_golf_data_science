Spirit Hollow Golf Course

Golf Course · 18 Holes

Course at a glance

Spirit Hollow Golf Course is an 18-hole golf course in Burlington, Iowa (52601). The course was designed by Rick Jacobson and opened in 1999. From the Gold tees, Spirit Hollow Golf Course plays to 7,301 yards at par 72. The course offers 7 sets of tees ranging from 5,056 to 7,301 yards, giving players of every ability a comfortable length. It carries a slope rating of 135 and a course rating of 75.9, comfortably above the 113 World Handicap System baseline and demanding accurate driving. That ranks it harder than roughly 84% of rated golf courses in Iowa. It is a public course open to daily-fee play. Walking is permitted. A pro shop is on site. The course is conditioned with bent grass greens and bent grass fairways. Hazards include 41-50 bunkers and water hazards in play. Tee times at Spirit Hollow Golf Course are booked through the course's official website or pro shop; Stymie does not show live tee-time inventory.

Course handicap is the number of handicap strokes received from that tee — it is not a prediction of what a golfer will score.
