Spirit Hollow Golf Course in Burlington, Iowa

5592 Clubhouse Dr, Burlington, Iowa, 52601-8675

Not far from Burlington, Spirit Hollow Golf Course offers terrific views and challenging play for golfers at every skill level. Well-groomed fairways and greens keep Spirit Hollow Golf Course difficult yet friendly, and the staff can offer tips and tricks for playing your best round. Spirit Hollow Golf Course is a terrific choice for your regular rounds of golf or a new destination for you and friends when visiting Burlington.
