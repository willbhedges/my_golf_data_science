 About Course
 Spirit Hollow Golf Course is a public 18-hole golf facility located in Burlington, Iowa. Designed by architect Rick Jacobson in 2000, the course features bent grass greens and fairways. The facility offers golfers a comprehensive practice environment with a driving range and on-site golf school. Visitors can also take advantage of instruction from a teaching professional. Situated in southeastern Iowa, Spirit Hollow provides a modern golfing experience for players seeking a well-maintained public course. The bent grass surfaces ensure consistent playing conditions across the greens and fairways, offering golfers a quality playing surface at this early 21st-century golf course.
