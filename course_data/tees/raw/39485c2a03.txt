 The 18-hole "Spirit Hollow" course 
 at the Spirit Hollow facility in Burlington , Iowa 
 features 6580 yards of golf from the longest tees for a par of 72. 
 The course rating is 73.6 and it has a slope rating of 129. 
 The estimated green fees for 18 holes is 
 $125. 
 Spirit Hollow Golf Club has an onsite driving range with 60 practice tees. 
 Designed by Richard Jacobson, ASGCA, the Spirit Hollow golf course 
 opened in 2000. 
 Estimated Green Fees
 $125 
 These rates are an estimate of what you might expect to pay at Spirit Hollow with a cart. Actual rates may vary. 
 Fees are typically lowest Monday through Thursday and during twilight (late afternoon and evenings). Expect to pay the highest rates on weekends.

 John W. 
 Reviewed July 30, 2017 
 One of the finest public courses you will ever play. Matches up to any country club quality. Course plays well from multiple tee boxes to accommodate all levels of play. Well manicured, tremendous driving range, putting green and overall practice area. Fantastic views and challenging and many signature type holes. Grounds crew and management are constantly working on upkeep and improvements. Highly recommend.

 adam J. 
 Reviewed October 01, 2010 
 Payed 53 on a wednesday, included cart and range. Service excellent. Friendly staff, informative without being overbearring. Great layout, tough but not overwhelming. A few WOW holes on the front, about 6 on the back.... only a few holes on the front were kinda blah, but totally acceptable... 3 of the par 5s are risk-reward holes, and a wide variety of par 4s... Best carts i have ever seen. GPS tells you everything, including how far you are from the tee, which helps alot when looking for balls in the rough... plus there are many yardage plates in the fairways if carts are on the path... the kind of place you would go again and again and again!!
