 Detailed description
 Spirit Hollow Golf Course, is a Public, 18 hole golf course located in Burlington, Iowa. 
 Spirit Hollow Golf Course first opened for play in June 2000. The par-72 course was designed by Richard Jacobsen. 
 Spirit Hollow Golf Course is located about an hour south of the quad cities (Davenport/Bettendorf/Rock Island/Moline). Designer Richard Jacobsen combined a variety of styles, from links to tree-lined parkland, and has skillfully woven them into this once heavily wooded area. 
 Golfers look down on long rolling fairways from numerous elevated tees. This par 72 course tops out at just over 7,000 yards.
