Designed by the legendary Tom Weiskopf, this 6,726-yard par 71 course offers an unforgettable golfing experience that seamlessly blends challenge and camaraderie. With meticulously manicured zoysia tees and fairways and generously sized bentgrass greens, it’s a course that caters to both precision and power in your game. As you step onto the first tee, you’ll immediately sense the aura of excitement that Old Kinderhook exudes. It’s a course that demands your best, pushing even the most seasoned golfers to their limits while maintaining an inviting and friendly atmosphere for all skill levels. Whether you’re a scratch golfer or a weekend enthusiast, you’ll find the perfect balance of competition and enjoyment here. Old Kinderhook’s reputation for drama and natural beauty is second to none. The course unfolds like a masterpiece, with valleys, cascading waterfalls, rolling hills, lush tree-lined fairways, and strategically placed water hazards that keep you on your toes. But what truly sets it apart is its stunning lakefront setting, a picturesque backdrop throughout your round. As you go through the course, the changing elevations will take your breath away, offering mesmerizing views at every turn. An Ozarks Golf Experience to Remember Each hole at Old Kinderhook tells a unique story, creating an engaging narrative that evolves with every swing. From the moment you tee off to the final putt, you’ll be immersed in a golfing adventure that’s as much about the surroundings as the game itself. The course isn’t just a golfing experience; it’s a journey through nature’s wonders, a symphony of challenges, and a visual feast for the eyes. So, whether you’re seeking a memorable golfing challenge or simply a day of unparalleled natural beauty, Old Kinderhook at the Lake of the Ozarks delivers it all. It’s a place where the spirit of golf and the majesty of Mother Nature come together, offering an unforgettable golfing experience that will leave you with lifelong memories. Come and test your skills, bask in the beauty, and embrace the thrill of Old Kinderhook – a true gem among America’s golf courses. Missouri Golf Resort Course Overview The 18-hole, par 71 championship golf course is open to the public year-round. It is the centerpiece of the 700+ acre Old Kinderhook master-planned community serving residents and guests. Routed through rugged Ozark hills, the course makes the most of nature’s beauty. A golf course must combine the land’s natural features with a design that challenges golfers of all skill levels to be among the best. Old Kinderhook has it all. This site’s elevation changes and topography assure Old Kinderhook a place among the finest courses in the United States. Hole By Hole Guide About Our Location Old Kinderhook Golf Course is located just 3 miles west of Camdenton on Hwy 54. Turn North onto Lake Road 54-80 and proceed to the front gates of Old Kinderhook. Follow Old Kinderhook Drive to the Golf Village area. The Golf Shop is located next to The Trophy Room. Golf Groups at Old Kinderhook Lake of the Ozarks Achieving personal milestones and goals in golf is incredibly rewarding, and our golf groups are here to support your journey. Whether you aspire to break an individual scoring record or simply enjoy the satisfaction of a well-struck shot, you’ll find encouragement and applause from your fellow group members. To book your golf group at Old Kinderhook, contact Blake Conklin . We can accommodate any size group. Joining one of our golf groups offers you the chance to immerse yourself in a vibrant community of golf enthusiasts, enhance your skills, and create cherished memories on and off the course. Whether you’re a seasoned golfer or new to the game, our golf groups provide an enriching and enjoyable experience you won’t miss. So, take the swing, make the putt, and join us for an unforgettable journey. Tee times are available all day, but please contact the Golf Shop at 573-319-3072 before scheduling. You can also reserve a tee time or golf lesson online. Old Kinderhook guests can secure advance tee times when booking a room. Keep the Game Exciting With Professional Lessons Golf is a game of constant improvement, and being part of a golf group can significantly enhance your skills. You’ll have the opportunity to learn from experienced golfers, receive valuable tips, and engage in friendly competitions that motivate you to become a better player. Individual, small group and clinic lessons are available. Dress Code Appropriate golf attire is required.
