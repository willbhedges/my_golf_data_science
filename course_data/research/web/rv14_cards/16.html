GOLF AT OLD KINDERHOOK

Nestled amidst the breathtaking beauty of the Lake of the Ozarks, Old Kinderhook Golf Course stands as one of America’s most spectacular golfing destinations. Designed by the legendary Tom Weiskopf, this 6,726-yard par 71 course offers an unforgettable golfing experience that seamlessly blends challenge and camaraderie. With meticulously manicured zoysia tees and fairways and generously sized bentgrass greens, it’s a course that caters to both precision and power in your game.

As you step onto the first tee, you’ll immediately sense the aura of excitement that Old Kinderhook exudes. It’s a course that demands your best, pushing even the most seasoned golfers to their limits while maintaining an inviting and friendly atmosphere for all skill levels. Whether you’re a scratch golfer or a weekend enthusiast, you’ll find the perfect balance of competition and enjoyment here.

Old Kinderhook’s reputation for drama and natural beauty is second to none. The course unfolds like a masterpiece, with valleys, cascading waterfalls, rolling hills, lush tree-lined fairways, and strategically placed water hazards that keep you on your toes. But what truly sets it apart is its stunning lakefront setting, a picturesque backdrop throughout your round. As you go through the course, the changing elevations will take your breath away, offering mesmerizing views at every turn.

An Ozarks Golf Experience to Remember

Each hole at Old Kinderhook tells a unique story, creating an engaging narrative that evolves with every swing. From the moment you tee off to the final putt, you’ll be immersed in a golfing adventure that’s as much about the surroundings as the game itself. The course isn’t just a golfing experience; it’s a journey through nature’s wonders, a symphony of challenges, and a visual feast for the eyes.

So, whether you’re seeking a memorable golfing challenge or simply a day of unparalleled natural beauty, Old Kinderhook at the Lake of the Ozarks delivers it all. It’s a place where the spirit of golf and the majesty of Mother Nature come together, offering an unforgettable golfing experience that will leave you with lifelong memories. Come and test your skills, bask in the beauty, and embrace the thrill of Old Kinderhook – a true gem among America’s golf courses.

Missouri Golf Resort Course Overview

The 18-hole, par 71 championship golf course is open to the public year-round. It is the centerpiece of the 700+ acre Old Kinderhook master-planned community serving residents and guests. Routed through rugged Ozark hills, the course makes the most of nature’s beauty. A golf course must combine the land’s natural features with a design that challenges golfers of all skill levels to be among the best. Old Kinderhook has it all. This site’s elevation changes and topography assure Old Kinderhook a place among the finest courses in the United States.

About Our Location

Old Kinderhook Golf Course is located just 3 miles west of Camdenton on Hwy 54. Turn North onto Lake Road 54-80 and proceed to the front gates of Old Kinderhook. Follow Old Kinderhook Drive to the Golf Village area. The Golf Shop is located next to The Trophy Room.

Amenities of Our Golf Resort in Missouri

Along with a practice green, 11-acre practice facility, short game and bunker practice areas, Old Kinderhook also features a Golf Shop with The Hook restaurant offering on-the-go menu items, three total Restaurants on-site, the 84 room Lodge hotel, Lake Area’s only Outdoor Ice Rink, Sand Volleyball Courts, Conference Center & Meeting Space, Wedding Facilities, an Outdoor Cabana with Fire Pits and Real Estate opportunities.

Golf Groups at Old Kinderhook Lake of the Ozarks

Achieving personal milestones and goals in golf is incredibly rewarding, and our golf groups are here to support your journey. Whether you aspire to break an individual scoring record or simply enjoy the satisfaction of a well-struck shot, you’ll find encouragement and applause from your fellow group members.

To book your golf group at Old Kinderhook, contact Blake Conklin. We can accommodate any size group. Joining one of our golf groups offers you the chance to immerse yourself in a vibrant community of golf enthusiasts, enhance your skills, and create cherished memories on and off the course. Whether you’re a seasoned golfer or new to the game, our golf groups provide an enriching and enjoyable experience you won’t miss. So, take the swing, make the putt, and join us for an unforgettable journey.

Tee times are available all day, but please contact the Golf Shop at 573-319-3072 before scheduling. You can also reserve a tee time or golf lesson online. Old Kinderhook guests can secure advance tee times when booking a room.

Keep the Game Exciting With Professional Lessons

Golf is a game of constant improvement, and being part of a golf group can significantly enhance your skills. You’ll have the opportunity to learn from experienced golfers, receive valuable tips, and engage in friendly competitions that motivate you to become a better player.

Individual, small group and clinic lessons are available.

Probably the most breathtaking and scenic hole and one of the favorites. The tee shot plays from at least 100 feet above the fairway to a narrow landing area. On the left of the green is a natural wet-lands and just behind a stand of hardwoods. It’s a very photogenic hole. Take your camera.

The green is well protected by two deep bunkers in front and to the right. Scores will vary from three to seven and a conservative approach should result in some easy pars or even birdies.

Number 12 also plays downhill and there’s lots of trouble off the tee to the left, including a stream and some large trees. The tee is as at least 100 feet above the landing area with a slight dog-leg to the left. the green has a very deep bunker on the left.

Par: 4

Yards: 437-401-351-320

Play VideoPlay Video

Number 13 plays along the lake shore. The semi-blind tee shot is over a hill with a blind bunker on the left. There’s no need to cut the corner of the dog-leg to gain a shorter shot. On your second shot, the right side gives you the best angle to the green. The landing area is wide but there are bunkers around the green and to the left. This is a very difficult par four.

Par: 5

Yards: 559-538-524-429

Play VideoPlay Video

#14 plays uphill toward the clubhouse. this is the longest hole at Old Kinderhook, with water along the entire left side. There is plenty of room on the right side of the landing area. Two bunkers protect the second landing area and the small green has additional bunkers left and right. This is a true three shot, par five.

This is another hole with water along the entire length. The water crosses the fairway at about 300 yards out. Play the second shot slightly up to an undulating and well protected green with bunkers on both sides.

A slight dog-leg to the right with bunkers right and left of the landing area. The second shot plays uphill to a semi-blind green, protected in front by a large and deep bunker. Avoid this bunker at all costs. It appears to be in front of the green, but is actually well short.

Par: 5

Yards: 516-476-442-406

Play VideoPlay Video

The most dramatic, memorable and scenic of the course. Big trees and a lake to the left and a deep bunker on the right side of the landing area.
