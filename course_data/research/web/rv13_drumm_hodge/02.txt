 Drumm Farm Golf Club 
 18 Holes 
 Driving Range 
 Bent Grass Greens 
 Zoysia Fairways & Teeing Grounds 
 Drumm Farm Golf Club is a beautifully manicured 18-hole golf course located in Independence, Missouri. Designed by renowned golf course architect, Craig Schreiner, the course features challenging holes that will test the skills of golfers of all levels. The course winds through rolling hills, forests, and wetlands, providing players with stunning views and a unique golfing experience. In addition to the golf course, Drumm Farm Golf Club offers a variety of amenities for golfers and non-golfers alike. The club has a practice facility with a driving range, putting green, and chipping area, perfect for golfers looking to improve their game. Whether you’re a serious golfer or simply looking for a unique and enjoyable day out, Drumm Farm Golf Club has something to offer. With its stunning scenery, challenging course, and commitment to community and sustainability, it’s no wonder that the club is one of the top golf destinations in the Independence area.

 Drumm Farm - Executive Golf Course 
 9 Holes 
 Par 3 Course 
 Bent Grass Greens 
 Zoysia Fairways & Teeing Grounds 
 Drumm Farm Golf Club’s Executive Course is located adjacent to the championship course. The 1674 yard, par 30 executive course is designed for players of all skill levels. The undulating layout has zoysia fairways, well-manicured bent grass greens, and three sets of tees. The Executive course is a great place for beginners to learn the game of golf and an even better place for other players to fine tune their skills.
