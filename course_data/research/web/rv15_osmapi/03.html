About The Course

Osage National Golf Resort

Osage National Golf Resort

  • 27 Holes
  • Driving Range
  • Bent Grass Greens

At Osage National Golf Resort, you can choose the way you play with our Links, River, and Mountain nines. The River and Mountain Nines have undergone renovations in 2025 and 2026. Videos will be updated shortly, but get a sneak peek of each nine below!

Links Nine

The rolling terrain and native grass areas give the feel of courses of old. Intermingled with the other two nines it is a great scoring opportunity. Relax and enjoy the ease at which this course is played.

Book Online

River Nine

Designed by Palmer in 1992, slight elevations and severe green side bunkering make this a nine to stand the test of time. Wildlife abounds as you enjoy the natural scenic beauty of these nine holes.

Book Online

Mountain Nine

The original front nine of the Palmer design, the elevated tees give way to majestic views of the river basin and the surrounding foliage. Shot control and placement are important as you work around these nine holes.
