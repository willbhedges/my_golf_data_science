 Golfing Gateway to the Ozarks 
 Eagle Knoll Golf Club is a par 72 course that sits on 180 acres of picturesque terrain. This classic Ozark style course features rolling zoysia fairways and sloping bent grass greens, with breathtaking elevation changes providing a golfing experience that is strategic as well as aesthetic. Winding through hills and gorges, wetlands and wildlife, Eagle Knoll offers four sets of tees to challenge all levels of play. 
 Holes: 18 | Par: 72 | Yardage: 6,920
