 Tournament Report 
 The NCCGA hosted our 1st golf tournament of the season in Central Region at Eagle Knoll Golf Club on Sep 26, 2015. Team University of Missouri-Columbia (A) won the division with a combined score of 772. The low individual score was recorded by Clayton Voss shooting a score of 146.
