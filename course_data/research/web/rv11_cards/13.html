Blue Springs, Missouri

The premier KC golf experience

Adams Pointe, the city of Blue Springs' 18 hole Championship Golf Course, is located in the rolling hills of Blue Springs, Missouri. A unique layout, skillfully carved out of woods and wetlands, superior playing conditions, and the friendly, professional staff consistently make Adams Pointe one of the premier choices for your Kansas City golf experience.
