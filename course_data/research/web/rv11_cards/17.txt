 The 18-hole "Adams Pointe" course 
 at the Adams Pointe Golf Club facility in Blue Springs , Missouri 
 features 6938 yards of golf from the longest tees for a par of 72. 
 The course rating is 73.8 and it has a slope rating of 131. 
 The estimated green fees for 18 holes is 
 $75. 
 Adams Pointe Golf Club Golf Club has an onsite driving range with 25 practice tees. 
 Designed by Donald Sechrest, the Adams Pointe golf course 
 opened in 1998. 
 Estimated Green Fees 
 $75 
 These rates are an estimate of what you might expect to pay at Adams Pointe with a cart. Actual rates may vary. 
 Fees are typically lowest Monday through Thursday and during twilight (late afternoon and evenings). Expect to pay the highest rates on weekends.
