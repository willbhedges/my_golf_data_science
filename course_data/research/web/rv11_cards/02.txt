Yes No Overview Activity Details Holes 18 Architect Floyd Farley Built 1959 Type n/a Access Visitors welcomed In 1959, the University of Missouri-Columbia opened the A.L. Gustin, Jr. Golf Course. The golf course is located 1/3 mile west of Faurot Field on Stadium Boulevard. A.L. Gustin, Jr. of Kansas City donated the land to the University of Missouri.
