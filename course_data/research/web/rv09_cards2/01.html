About our golf course

Sycamore Ridge is like two superb golf courses in one. The steep, wooded hillsides, valleys and streams of the front nine lead you to the gently rolling, links-style back nine. The full experience challenges your skills even as it calms your spirit. It’s no wonder Golf Digest has named Sycamore Ridge the number-one course in Kansas City year after year and Zagat has added the course to its list of the finest in the United States. Sycamore Ridge is golf at its very best.
