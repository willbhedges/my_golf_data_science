About Us/Scorecard

Golf Course

The par-70 layout at Lake Shawnee Golf Course in Topeka, Kansas measures over 6300 yards from the back tees. It was designed by Larry W. Flatt in 1972 and updated by Craig Schreiner.

Many holes of the golf course bring together breathtaking views and the challenges of playing alongside Lake Shawnee. The manageable length of our golf course and affordable anytime rates make Lake Shawnee Golf Course your best choice for outdoor recreation.

Take a few moments to explore our website and you will discover why so many golfers call Lake Shawnee their home course.

See you on the course soon!
