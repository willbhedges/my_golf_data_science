 About Our Golf Course 
 Shoal Creek Golf Course is a public course that offers superior course conditions and unmatched customer service. The practice area includes a 34 station driving range, short-game practice area and putting green. The beautiful clubhouse is the perfect place for weddings, banquets, parties or meetings.

 Why play golf at Shoal Creek? 
 The Course 
 Upscale Championship Public ‘Daily Fee' Golf Course 
 Built in 2001 by architect Steve Wolford 
 Owned by Kansas City Park and Recreation 
 Operated by Orion Management Solutions 
 Par 71, Yardage 6,952, Course Rating 74.1, Slope 140 
 6 sets of tees offer yardages for golfers of all abilities 
 Club Car Carts equipped with Fairway IQ GPS yardage & navigation system.  
 Zoysia grass tees & fairways, Penn G-2 Bent grass greens 
 The Clubhouse & Players Grill 
 11,000 sq. ft. state-of -the-art clubhouse; built in October 2003 
 Features 4 main rooms: the Golf Shop, Fireplace Lounge, Players Grill, and the Ballroom.  
 Each area is enclosed by retractable doors and offers the option of several dining areas 
 All retractable doors can be opened up for one large banquet area accommodating 200 guests 
 Designed to attract public golfers, corporate and charity golf outings and social affairs 
 An outdoor, covered patio overlooks the driving range which can also seat up to 40 guests 
 The Practice Center 
 2 tier, 12,000 sq. ft. Zoysia grass teeing area 
 34 hitting stations; 6 target greens; 6,000 sq. ft. putting green 
 Private Lesson areas. Group, Ladies' and Junior Lessons available 
 The Accolades 
 Ranked #2 Public Course in Missouri by Golf Week's America's Best You Can Play 
 4-Star Rated by Golf Digest ‘Best Places to Play' 
 Rated 5-Stars in the KC Metro area by Tee-Times Golf Guide 
 Ranked the #1 Public Course in Missouri by the Golf Card Traveler 
 2026 Host to the Central Links Junior Championship 
 2026 Host to the Midwest PGA Senior/Junior/Assistant Championship 
 2026 Host to the Watson Challenge 
 Featured in Links Magazine 'Top 10 Missouri Courses You Can Play'
