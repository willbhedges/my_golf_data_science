About Bandon Dunes Golf Resort

Bandon Dunes Golf Resort is a golf club in Coos County, Oregon, United States. Course layouts include Bandon Dunes, Bandon Preserve, Brandon Trails, Pacific Dunes, Sheep Ranch, Shorty's. Each course offers a unique golfing experience, from traditional links layouts to inland forested designs, all set against the backdrop of the Pacific Ocean.

Course details

  • Number of holes: 18
  • Par: 72
  • Tees available: 7

About this course

The Bandon Dunes course is an 18-hole par 72 course.

The scorecard has 4 par 3s, 10 par 4s and 4 par 5s.

The hardest hole by stroke index is the 5th (par 4, stroke index 1).

The Black tees have a course rating of 74.1 and a slope rating of 143.

The Green tees have a course rating of 71.4 and a slope rating of 133.

The White tees have a course rating of 70.7 and a slope rating of 127.

The Gold tees have a course rating of 69.5 and a slope rating of 133.

The Red tees have a course rating of 72.1 and a slope rating of 127.

The Orange tees have a course rating of 72.4 and a slope rating of 128.

The Royal Blue tees have a course rating of 61.5 and a slope rating of 101.
