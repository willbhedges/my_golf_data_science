 About this course 
 The Bandon Dunes course is an 18-hole par 72 course. The scorecard has 4 par 3s, 10 par 4s and 4 par 5s. The hardest hole by stroke index is the 5th (par 4, stroke index 1). The Black tees have a course rating of 74.1 and a slope rating of 143. The Green tees have a course rating of 71.4 and a slope rating of 133. The White tees have a course rating of 70.7 and a slope rating of 127. The Gold tees have a course rating of 69.5 and a slope rating of 133. The Red tees have a course rating of 72.1 and a slope rating of 127. The Orange tees have a course rating of 72.4 and a slope rating of 128. The Royal Blue tees have a course rating of 61.5 and a slope rating of 101.
