 About this course 
 The Links course is an 18-hole par 72 course. It measures 7,311 yards from the Purple tees, 6,574 yards from the Orange tees, 6,574 yards from the Orange (L) tees, 6,015 yards from the White tees, 6,015 yards from the White (L) tees, 4,843 yards from the Teal tees and 4,843 yards from the Teal (L) tees. The scorecard has 4 par 3s, 10 par 4s and 4 par 5s. The longest hole is the 12th (par 5, 575 yards), while the shortest is the 16th (par 3, 181 yards). The hardest hole by stroke index is the 7th (par 4, 472 yards, stroke index 1). The Purple tees have a course rating of 75.2 and a slope rating of 142. The Orange tees have a course rating of 73.2 and a slope rating of 135. The Orange (L) tees have a course rating of 78.1 and a slope rating of 143. The White tees have a course rating of 69.1 and a slope rating of 123. The White (L) tees have a course rating of 74.7 and a slope rating of 138. The Teal tees have a course rating of 63.7 and a slope rating of 110. The Teal (L) tees have a course rating of 68.4 and a slope rating of 112.
