It is a public golf course that provides an enjoyable and challenging experience for golfers of all skill levels. The golf course was named after Fred Arbanas, a former professional football player who played for the Kansas City Chiefs in the National Football League (NFL). Arbanas was also a prominent figure in the Lee's Summit community, having served as a Jackson County legislator for over two decades. The course spans over 6,500 yards and offers a variety of tee options, allowing golfers to tailor their game to their skill level. These features not only enhance the aesthetics of the course but also add to the strategic challenges that golfers face, making the game even more enjoyable. Apart from the golf course, Fred Arbanas Golf Course offers additional amenities such as a driving range, practice putting green, and a fully stocked pro shop. The pro shop offers a wide range of golfing equipment, clothing, and accessories to cater to the needs of golfers. The club also has a clubhouse that provides a comfortable and inviting atmosphere for golfers to relax and socialize. The clubhouse features a bar and grill where golfers can enjoy delicious food and drinks before or after their round of golf.  About this course 
 The Championship course is an 18-hole par 72 course. The scorecard has 4 par 3s, 10 par 4s and 4 par 5s. The hardest hole by stroke index is the 1st (par 4, stroke index 1). The Blue tees have a course rating of 73.2 and a slope rating of 131. The White tees have a course rating of 70.7 and a slope rating of 126. The Gold tees have a course rating of 68.5 and a slope rating of 121. The Red tees have a course rating of 72.1 and a slope rating of 125.
