The 18-hole TPC course at the TPC Louisiana facility in Avondale, features 7,010 yards of golf from the longest tees for a par of 72. The course rating is 73.9 and it has a slope rating of 135 on Bermuda grass. Designed by Pete Dye, ASGCA, the TPC golf course opened in 2019. PGA TOUR Golf Course Properties, Inc manages this facility, with Luke Farabaugh as the General Manager.
