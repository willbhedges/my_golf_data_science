Phone: 913-826-3570
HOURS: In Season: 6:30 am – Sunset

Heritage Park Golf Course is one of the most beautiful courses in the Kansas City metropolitan area. It offers an exceptional combination of traditional tree-lined and links-type holes which provide an interesting, challenging and picturesque game. Three lakes, as well as Coffee Creek, bring water into play on 11 of the 18 holes. 

Heritage Park Golf Course is comprised of nearly 7,000 irrigated yards which play to a par 71 with the finishing holes providing excellent viewing for our galleries during tournament play. Our course is lined with Zoysia grass fairways and beautiful Bent grass greens which provide great golf conditions year-round. Also, our newly expanded Zoysia grass driving range and the expansive putting clock make the facility a great place to practice as well.

The golf entrance is accessible from the park’s west side off South Black Bob Road.
