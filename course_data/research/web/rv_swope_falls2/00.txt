 About our golf course 
 Following an extensive restoration that returned Swope Memorial Golf Course to A.W. Tillinghast's original design, Kansas City's Home Course enters a new era while honoring its rich history. Carved through the wooded landscape of Swope Park, the course features a tight, strategic layout with rolling fairways and undulating greens that provide a true test of golf. Sweeping views of the downtown Kansas City skyline, paired with the unmistakable roars of the lions from the Kansas City Zoo below, create a golfing experience unlike any other in the city.

 Why play golf at Swope Memorial? 
 Unique setting 
 Skyline views of downtown, accompanied by the roars of lions from the K.C. Zoo below, give this course character that is unmatched anywhere in the city. 
 Historic design 
 Originally opened in 1915, the course was completely re-designed by A.W. Tillinghast in 1934. In 2025, the course went through a complete historic restoration to bring it back to Tillinghast's original intent.
