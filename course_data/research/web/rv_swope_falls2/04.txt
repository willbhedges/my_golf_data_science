 Swope Memorial Golf Course at a glance 
 Swope Memorial Golf Course is an 18-hole golf course
 in Kansas City, MO, USA
 with a total par of 72 (front-9 36, back-9 36).
 Swope Memorial Golf Course opened in 1915 as Kansas City's first public course, set within the parkland of Swope Park. It was rebuilt in 1934 to a complete redesign by A. W. Tillinghast, the noted architect behind Winged Foot and Baltusrol. The course hosted the Kansas City Open on the PGA Tour in 1949, the only public course in the area to stage a Tour event, and it later held the United Golf Association National Championship and a USGA Women's Amateur Public Links Championship.
 The layout combines winding, tree lined fairways with sharp changes in elevation, zoysia grass surfaces, undulating greens and a scattering of sand and grass bunkers, rewarding placement over raw power on several holes. Elevated tees and blind approaches give the round a testing character, and a handful of holes open onto views across to the downtown Kansas City skyline. Visitors consistently praise the course's condition, value and variety, with several holes singled out as memorable enough to want to play again.
