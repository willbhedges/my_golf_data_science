 Swope Memorial Golf Course in Kansas City is a 18-hole course playing to a par of 72 and roughly 6,237 yards measured tee-to-green by GPS.
 Walking the course covers about 4.2 miles (roughly 8,400 steps) including the walks between greens and tees. Its greens average 32.7 yards deep front-to-back.
