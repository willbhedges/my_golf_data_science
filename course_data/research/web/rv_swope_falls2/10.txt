 The Falls Golf Club at a glance 
 The Falls Golf Club is an 18-hole golf course
 in O'Fallon, MO, USA
 with a total par of 71 (front-9 36, back-9 35).
 As well as the The Falls course, The Falls Golf Club also has the The Falls Golf Club course. 
 The Falls Golf Club opened in 1994 on the outskirts of O'Fallon, Missouri, a short drive from St Louis. The 18 hole, par 71 layout runs to around 6,510 yards over rolling, tree lined ground shaped by lakes, creeks and a series of waterfalls that give the club its name. It has since hosted PGA, MGA and USGA events, and offers a full practice facility alongside the course itself.
 The golf combines narrow zoysia fairways with bentgrass greens, water in play on many holes and a back nine generally regarded as more forgiving than a demanding front nine. The short par 4 sixteenth, at around 320 yards, stands out as a quirky, strategic test framed by one of the course's waterfalls. Visitors consistently praise the abundance of water features, saying they give the course an exotic feel that belies its suburban Missouri setting.
