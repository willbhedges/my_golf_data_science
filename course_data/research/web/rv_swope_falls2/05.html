Swope Memorial Golf Course in Kansas City is a 18-hole course playing to a par of 72 and roughly 6,237 yards measured tee-to-green by GPS. Walking the course covers about 4.2 miles (roughly 8,400 steps) including the walks between greens and tees. Its greens average 32.7 yards deep front-to-back. The Caddie Tap app carries hole-by-hole GPS for every hole here — live distance to the green with front, middle and back numbers, a caddie club suggestion from your own carry distances, and one-tap scoring from your iPhone or Apple Watch.
