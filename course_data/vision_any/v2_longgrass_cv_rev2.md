# Long grass on the automatic map (rev 2): leave-one-course-out over the 11 truth courses

**Reading (by hand, after Actions run 40949758028).**

- **Not adopted.** Out of fold the result is 129/151, against 130/151 with no long grass; Mozingo loses a hole
  (11 -> 10). Chosen on all 11 courses, `t0.3_relaxed_d20_g0.04` reaches 132/151, but that is in sample and does
  not hold up out of fold. model_v2/model.json and the packages are unchanged.
- **Corridor-focused training did not fix recall.** Out of fold at P >= 0.5, corridor recall is still 0.01-0.05 on the
  courses with real native grass (Spirit Hollow, Mozingo, Coldwater). The four new courses have no hand-mapped
  native grass at all, so they add only negatives.

Generated by `course_data/vision_any/v2_longgrass_cv.py` (workflow `.github/workflows/longgrass_cv.yml`). Classifier: the v2 long-grass LightGBM (multi-year NAIP RGB + NIR, texture, lidar height / low-return fractions / roughness, terrain, OSM turf distances) trained for each test course on the hand maps of the other 10. Rev 2: corridor-focused classifier, trained only on the hand maps' landing-corridor cells 145-440 yds off the tee, with seasonal NAIP contrast, NIR texture, lidar 0.2-1.5 m height share, mowing-pattern edges and OSM grassland / heath / rough polygons added (`add_rev2_features`). Each setting: the committed package with its vision zones replaced (trees = the product's line, lidar canopy eroded 1 m; water as committed), OB re-inferred, product quick decision (n = 8000) per tee shot vs the hand truth (scoreboard rows; Eagle Knoll and Gateway through the same hand-map scorer, Back tees). Setting ids: `t<P(native) threshold>_<strict|relaxed>_d<min yds from OSM fairway>_g<course share gate>`; `off` = no long grass (the product).
