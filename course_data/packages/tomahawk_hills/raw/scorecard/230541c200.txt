 Contact us: Tomahawk Hills Golf Course 
17501 Midland Drive
Shawnee, KS 66217
Phone: 913-631-8000
 Come play Tomahawk Hills! Our course is ranked as one of the area’s most challenging public courses. It offers patrons an exciting 18-hole championship course of over 6,000 yards which plays to a par-70. With beautiful Zoysia grass fairways and driving range, and Bent grass greens, this historic and challenging course is ready for the modern golfer. Set your  tee time today online , or call our clubhouse!
