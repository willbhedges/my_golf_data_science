 Take A Swing
 Join us at Mozingo Lake Golf Course, where every swing is a step closer to perfection against the backdrop of stunning natural beauty. Nestled within the sprawling landscape of Mozingo Lake Recreation Park, our 18-hole championship course offers a golfing experience like no other. Designed by world-renowned architect, Donald Sechrest, our meticulously crafted fairways and greens provide a challenge for players of all skill levels, from seasoned pros to casual enthusiasts. Immerse yourself in the tranquility of the surroundings as you navigate our scenic layout, featuring strategically placed bunkers, water hazards, and lush foliage.
 For a quick round or to hone your short game, don't miss our Watson 9, a fantastic addition to our course. These nine holes offer a unique experience, perfect for players looking for a shorter yet equally enjoyable game.
 After your round, relax and unwind at our pro shop where you can find everything you need for a great game and refreshing beverages. Whether you're here for a leisurely game with friends or a competitive tournament, Mozingo Lake Golf Course promises an unforgettable golfing experience that will keep you coming back for more.

 Refine Your Skills 
 Interested in mastering the game or refining your golfing skills? Look no further! PGA Head Golf Pro Kyle Easter offers personalized lessons suitable for all ages and skill levels. Lesson packages include range balls for warm-up and instruction. Plus, take advantage of optional video swing analysis and access to our swing bay facilities for enhanced learning. Start your journey to golfing excellence with us today!
