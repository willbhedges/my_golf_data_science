 Course Information 
 Situated on the highest point in Cass County, our rolling hills are dotted with lush woodlands and natural wetlands, making Creekmoor the premiere choice for a fun and unique Kansas City golf experience. Located just 20 minutes south of Downtown, we are conveniently located in the Southeast Metro. 
 ​ 
 Our friendly and knowledgeable staff looks forward to hosting you at The Golf Club at Creekmoor! 
 ​ 
 History 
 Creekmoor’s immaculately groomed bentgrass tees, lush zoysia fairways, and firm, fast bentgrass greens were designed by landscape architect and ASCGA member Tom Clark. 
 The course offers a true championship challenge for the serious golfer. It is the perfect venue for a social round for couples, a relaxed and comfortable track for seniors or a stimulating discovery for youngsters just trying the game on for size. 
 Read Tom Clark’s hole-by-hole narrative for an in-depth look at the course.

 Practice Facilities 
 Our practice facility includes a putting green, chipping green and practice tees. Player development, junior golf, clinics, fittings and golf club manufacturer fitting and demo days also take place on the practice tees. The area is open year round, weather permitting, and maintains the same hours of operation as the golf course.

 Superintendent 
 Tim Nielsen 
 t.nielsen@creekmoorpoa.com 
 Our Golf Course Superintendent, Tim Nielsen is a “Class A” member of the
Golf Course Superintendents Association of America. Lucrative Class A status
is earned through a combination of formal education, experience, continuing
education, and passing the GCSAA IPM Exam. Class A membership
demonstrates personal commitment to lifelong learning, environmental
stewardship, and elevating golf course management. We are honored to have
Tim leading our experienced grounds crew here at Creekmoor.
Tim graduated from the University of Nebraska-Lincoln with a Bachelor of
Science degree in horticulture and turfgrass management. His career began
when he assisted in the construction of Drumm Farm Golf Club in
Independence, Missouri. Once the construction of Drumm Farm was
complete, he became the assistant superintendent for grow-in and golf
course maintenance. Soon after he was promoted to golf course
superintendent until he took over his current role at Creekmoor in 2011.
He has served on the board of directors of the Heart of America Golf Course
Superintendents Association for many years including serving as the
association’s president twice. In his career as a golf course superintendent,
Tim has devoted extensive time and effort in promoting the game of golf and
the art of the greens-keeping and for those efforts he was awarded the
prestigious Chester H. Mendenhall award by the HAGCSA in 2015.
Tim decided upon a career in golf course maintenance because of his passion
for the game of golf. Of the many notable golf courses he’s had the privilege
of experiencing, a few stick out to him as the best he ever played. He enjoyed
the pristine conditioning and hospitality of Sage Valley in Augusta, GA and
the formidable test of Pinehurst #2 in North Carolina. But his overall favorite
golf experiences happen at Ballyneal in Holyoke, CO. He describes Ballyneal
“as pure as golf could possibly be”.
His favorite PGA golfers are Justin Thomas, Jordan Spieth, Rory McElroy and
Dustin Johnson, but he thinks Tiger Woods is still the most enjoyable. Of
course, to Tim the most enjoyable part of watching professional golf isn’t
about the golfers. He truly loves learning about the golf course itself,
examining the architecture, the conditioning and seeing how those aspects
impact the way the golfers attack the course.
 PGA Head Golf Professional 
 Joe Taylor 
 j.taylor@creekmoorpoa.com 
 This is Head Golf Pro Joe Taylor’s fifth season at The Golf Club at Creekmoor, after
spending over 22 years at upscale facilities such as Loch Lloyd in Missouri,
Minnesota’s North Oaks Golf Club, Prestwick and The Minikahda Club, and Arizona’s
Estancia Club and Red Mountain Ranch Country Club.
He is currently the Head Golf Coach at The Barstow School in Kansas City for both
the Boys and Girls Programs. Joe inherited a program that had never seen much
success; under his tenure they have won two Missouri Class 2 Team State
Championships and he has coached multiple players to All-District and All-State
accolades. With over two decades of teaching experience, Joe enjoys helping golfers
of all levels.
His favorite golf experience was playing Teeth of the Dog at the Casa deCampo
resort in the Dominican Republic.
Joe’s favorite aspect of being a golf pro is the daily interaction with so many different
people. He enjoys Creekmoor’s course for the variety of holes and not having the
same shot over and over again. When he isn’t on the course, you can find him
binge-watching movies and spending time with family.
