 Programs 
 Our beautifully maintained 18-hole course offers a unique challenge on each hole, with scenic views, strategic layouts, and helpful tips to enhance your game. To ensure an enjoyable experience for all, we kindly ask players to follow our course rules, including pace of play, cart path etiquette, and dress code. For an outline of our daily rates and membership options, please visit our Rates page. Tee times can be reserved easily online or by calling our clubhouse, and we recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred slot. We also host a variety of events throughout the year, from tournaments to family-friendly activities, so be sure to check our Events page regularly. If you’re looking for ways to support our course, consider making a gift or pledge to help us continue providing an outstanding golfing experience. We look forward to welcoming you to Veenker Memorial golf Course!
