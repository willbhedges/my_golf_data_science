 Golf
 The 18-hole, William Langford-designed golf course overlooks downtown Des Moines and is consistently ranked best in the metro, state and midwest. A traditionally designed course with mature trees and hills, the course plays differently every time you step up to the tee box.

 Dining
 A variety of dining experiences offer ample opportunities to enjoy the Clubhouse with family and friends. Private and intimate experiences, casual al fresco dining, Sunday dinner, or celebrating special occasions are all enjoyed with exceptional dining and service at the forefront. 
 DISCOVER DINING 
 Tennis
 Home to exceptionally cared for clay courts and two omni courts, all ages and abilities enjoy the tennis experience. Open play, clinics, leagues and instruction are all offered for youth and adults  as well as pickleball!
 EXPLORE COURTS 
 Aquatics
 Three pools keep members cool, as do the drinks from the Tiki Bar and ice cream treats in the Snack Bar. Member parties, Friday night movies and swim instruction make the pools lively, and complimentary poolside yoga on Sundays closes your pool week on a relaxing note!

 Wakonda History
 2022 marks Wakonda Clubs centennial. Through 100 years, Wakonda has stayed loyal to its generations of member families and its neighborhood communities. Hosting nationally recognized golf and tennis competitive and philanthropic tournaments and unique events has kept Wakonda as one of the storied social spots in Des Moines history.
