                             — LOCAL RULES —
• Please maintain a Pace of Play of 4½ hours or less per 18 holes.
• Please keep up with the group ahead of you and play without delay.
• Do Not Enter the Buffalo areas, this is Strictly Prohibited for your safety.
• Course Guides are out on the course to encourage appropriate pace of play and
  your cooperation is appreciated.

  lightning & dangerous conditions and seek shelter immediately.
• Golfers are responsible for any injury or damage resulting from the golfer’s actions
  on a golf course or the property including errant shots.
