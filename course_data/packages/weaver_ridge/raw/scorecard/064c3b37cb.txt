 18 Holes of Championship Caliber Golf! 
 Weaver Ridge Golf Club provides 18 Holes of Championship-Caliber Golf in Illinois. The course plays to exactly 7000 yards, with a USGA Rating of 73.1 with a Slope of 140. There are 5 sets of tees, allowing players of all skill levels to enjoy their round or seek a challenge. Highly heralded and designed by the master team of Hurdzan-Fry, Weaver Ridge is surely a golf course that needs to be on your bucket list.
 We are proud to have received these notable awards, among many others:
